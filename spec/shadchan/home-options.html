Which first screen would help you most?

We are building a phone app for shadchanim. It keeps your singles, your shidduchim and your notes in one place, and it tells you who you still need to call back. Everything stays on your own phone.

Below are four ideas for the first screen you see when you open the app. They all show the same made-up morning, just arranged differently. The bar at the bottom is the same in all four: Home, Guys, Girls, Shidduchim, Contacts.

Please look at all four, then answer the short questions at the end.
