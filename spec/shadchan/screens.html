Shadchan Mode: screen sketches, draft 2

Twelve sketches of the proposal in spec/shadchan/design.md, in the same dark look as Single Mode. These are sketches to react to, not the finished screens. All names are fictional.

New in this draft: one app with a mode switch, Guys and Girls as their own tabs, Home kept from Single Mode with its notes, follow-ups and history, and the Single Mode words (Home, Shidduchim).

How to give feedback: each block has a name in blue (for example home.your-move). Tell me the name and what to change, the same way as in Tweak mode.
